On the shopper's garment page, when do they pick the size and colour?

Shopper's phone, arriving from Shopee. Both options keep today's page (photo, name, Fit / Colour / Fabric tabs) and add the two pickers. The shopper always chooses. We never pick for them. Click the one that feels right.
